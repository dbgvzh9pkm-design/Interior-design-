# How to build the walnut bed frame

Queen platform bed for a **160 × 200 cm** mattress, as in
`designs/…bare-bed-frame.jpg`. Dimensions are in `plan/bed-frame-sketch.png`.

**Finished size:** 167 cm wide × 209 cm long. Headboard 110 cm high, rail top
30 cm, slats at 25 cm, mattress top about 50 cm.

**Time:** about 2 days, including stain drying. **Skill:** basic DIY, or give this
sheet to a carpenter.

---

## 1. Materials (cut list)

| Qty | Part | Size (cm) | Material |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Headboard panel | 157 × 95 × 4 (fits between the two head legs; or 2 × 1.8 cm sheets glued) | Walnut-veneer plywood, or solid walnut boards glued edge to edge |
| 2 | Head legs | 5 × 5 × 110 (corner posts either side of the headboard) | Solid walnut or oak, stained |
| 2 | Foot legs | 5 × 5 × 30, tapered to 3.5 cm at the floor for the bottom 15 cm | Solid walnut or oak |
| 2 | Side rails | 199 × 15 × 3 (between head legs and foot legs) | Solid walnut or oak |
| 1 | Foot rail | 157 × 15 × 3 (between the two foot legs) | Solid walnut or oak |
| 1 | Centre beam | 201 × 9 × 4.5 | Pine or spruce (hidden) |
| 2 | Centre legs | 5 × 5 × 14 | Pine (hidden) |
| 2 | Support strips (ledgers) | 199 × 3 × 3 (same as the side rails) | Pine |
| 15 | Slats | 161 × 7 × 1.8 | Pine or birch |

**Hardware:**
- 4 × **bed-rail brackets** (hook-in type), so the bed comes apart to move it and fits through the 80 cm corridor
- About 40 × 4 × 40 mm wood screws (ledgers and slats)
- 8 × 5 × 70 mm screws (headboard to head legs)
- 2 × 8 mm dowels or 2 metal corner braces (centre beam to head and foot)
- Wood glue, 6 felt pads

**Finish:** dark walnut stain (skip it if you use real walnut), then 2 coats of matte
**hardwax oil** or water-based matte polyurethane.

**Tools:** drill/driver, jigsaw or circular saw (or have the timber yard cut
everything to size), sander with 120 and 180 grit, clamps, tape measure, square, level.

> Easiest route: order every piece **cut to size** at the timber yard using the
> table above. Then it's only sanding, staining and screwing.

---

## 2. Steps

**1. Sand and stain everything first.**
- Sand all visible parts with 120 grit, then 180 grit.
- Round every edge very slightly.
- Stain, then oil or varnish. Two thin coats, sanding lightly between them.
- Let it dry overnight.

**2. Make the headboard.**
- Stand the two **110 cm head legs** 157 cm apart, so the outside width is 167 cm.
- Fit the **157 cm headboard panel** between them, flush with the back of the legs. Its bottom edge sits **15 cm** off the floor and its top is level with the leg tops at 110 cm.
- Glue, then screw through each leg into the panel edge: 4 × 70 mm screws per leg, or use dowels for no visible screws.

**3. Taper the foot legs.**
- Mark 15 cm up from the bottom on each foot leg.
- The taper runs from that mark down to 3.5 cm at the floor, on the two inside faces.
- Cut the taper, then sand.

**4. Fit the rail brackets.**
- On each end of both **199 cm** side rails, screw the hook half of a bed-rail bracket. Keep each rail flush with the outer face of the legs.
- On the head legs and foot legs, fit the matching plates so that the **rail top sits at 30 cm** from the floor, meaning the rail spans 15 to 30 cm.

**5. Build the foot end.**
- Glue and screw the **foot rail (157 cm)** between the two foot legs, flush with their outside faces, at 15 to 30 cm high.
- Use 2 × 70 mm screws per end through the legs, or pocket screws.

**6. Fix the ledgers.**
- Screw a **3 × 3 cm support strip** along the inside of each side rail.
- Its top edge sits **6.8 cm below the rail top**: 5 cm of drop for the mattress plus the 1.8 cm slat. That puts the slats at 25 cm.
- Use 4 × 40 mm screws every 25 cm.

**7. Stand it up.**
- Hook the side rails onto the headboard legs and the foot end.
- Check it's square: the two diagonals should measure the same.

**8. Fit the centre beam.**
- Run the **centre beam** down the middle, head to foot.
- Its top must be level with the top of the ledgers.
- Rest it on the **two 14 cm centre legs**, placed at roughly one-third and two-thirds of the length.
- Fix the ends to the headboard and foot rail with dowels or corner braces.

**9. Lay the slats.**
- Lay the **15 slats** across, starting 1 cm from the headboard.
- Keep the gaps even at about 6.8 cm, and never more than 7 cm, or the mattress sags.
- Use a 6.8 cm spacer block.
- Screw each slat once at each end into the ledgers and once into the centre beam.

**10. Finish off.**
- Put felt pads under all six legs to protect your floor.
- Drop in the 160 × 200 mattress. It should have about 0.5 cm of play on each side.

---

## 3. Checks before using it

- [ ] Rail top at 30 cm and slats at 25 cm, all round
- [ ] Both diagonals equal (frame is square)
- [ ] No slat gap bigger than 7 cm
- [ ] Centre legs touch the floor (no rocking)
- [ ] It doesn't wobble when you push the headboard

**Rough cost:**
- About **$250–450** in materials, with walnut-veneer ply and oak rails
- About **$600–1,000** in solid walnut
- If a carpenter builds it, add their labour.
