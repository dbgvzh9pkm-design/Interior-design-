    Measure these to make it exact

    1. Room width, TV wall to bed wall, at floor level.
    2. Room length, door wall to window wall.
    3. Ceiling height.
    4. Window: width, height, sill height, and distance from the TV-wall corner.
    5. Entry door: width, distance from the TV-wall corner, and which way it swings.
    6. Bathroom glass box: width and depth, and where its glass door is and how far it swings.
    7. Where the ceiling light point, the sockets and the TV aerial or internet point are.
    Clearances follow common guidance: 30–45 cm between a sofa and its coffee table, and at least 60–75 cm for a walkway.
