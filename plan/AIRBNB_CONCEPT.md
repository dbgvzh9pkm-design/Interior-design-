# Airbnb studio: designer concept

**Room:** 302 × 614 cm, ceiling 287 cm. Glass bathroom box by the door, small
high window at the far end, existing dark wood floor (kept).

**Idea:** a *warm boutique-hotel studio*. Bright enough to photograph well for
the listing, calm enough to sleep in, and practical for up to 4 guests.

- Images: `designs/…airbnb-concept-door.jpg` (and `-v2`)
- Layout: `plan/airbnb-concept-plan.png`

---

## 1. Three zones (door → window)

| Zone | What's there | Why guests care |
|---|---|---|
| **Arrive** (corridor + bathroom) | Full-length mirror and hooks in the corridor. Bathroom glass wrapped in a ceiling-track curtain. | Privacy, somewhere to drop keys and coats, and a mirror check before going out. |
| **Live + Work** (middle) | **Sofa bed** 180 × 90 on the right wall. Round coffee table and floor lamp. **Desk** 100 × 45 with chair and lamp on the left wall. **TV on a swivel stand** that turns to the sofa or the bed. **Open wardrobe** (rail, shelf, luggage rack) nearest the door. | Sofa bed = sleeps **4** (higher nightly rate). Desk = remote workers. Wardrobe = the top complaint in small rentals is "nowhere to put clothes". |
| **Sleep** (window end) | **Queen bed centred** on the window wall, headboard under the window, **68 cm** of walkway on both sides. Nightstands and plug-in wall lights on both sides. **Full-width blackout curtain** across the whole wall. | Two guests can each get in and out of their own side, with their own light and charger. The curtain wall hides the small high window and blacks out the room. |

| Textiles | Oatmeal linen blackout curtains (290 cm drop), white bedding, olive throw, cream wool rug | 2 sets of bedding |
| Metal | Brass wall lights and rail, black desk lamp and chair | – |

## 3. Lighting (all plug-in, nothing wired into the walls)

- **Ceiling:** linen drum flush-mount, dimmable 2700K bulbs
- **Bed:** 2 brass plug-in wall lights, one each side
- **Desk:** black task lamp
- **Sofa:** linen floor lamp
- Evenings run on lamps only, for a warm "hotel" mood

## 4. Guest essentials

- Blackout curtains, 2 pillows per guest, a mattress protector
- USB/USB-C chargers at both nightstands and the desk
- 20 hangers, luggage rack, hooks, full-length mirror
- Smart TV (guests log into their own apps), fast Wi-Fi info card
- Smoke/CO alarm, fire extinguisher, smart lock
- Kettle tray on the desk, or on a side shelf if there's no kitchen

## 6. Photography tip for the listing

Shoot at dusk with every lamp on and the curtains half open. Take these shots:
1. From the door (the view in the render)
2. From the bed toward the sofa
3. Details: the desk, the wardrobe and the bedside
